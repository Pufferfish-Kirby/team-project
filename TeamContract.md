# Team Contract

## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---

## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* **Platform:** We will be using WhatsApp for communication outside of class.
* **Response time:** Everyone should try to respond to messages by the end of the day. If it is something that doesn't necessarily need a reply, react to the message so people know you read it.
* **What to notify each other about:** We should notify each other about potential conflicts, which includes assignment, lecture, and tutorial conflicts. It also includes unforeseen circumstances (ex: getting sick, family emergency, etc.), and not being able to figure something out, and once you finish something/when you make a pull request.
* **Respectful and inclusive behaviour:** It is important for all of us to think about how our words affect others, to make sure everyone feel heard and understood. We make sure that everyone is able to say their thoughts and opinions, and no one treats anyone with disrespect

### Other Norms and Expectations

* Being at tutorial by 8:10 and ensuring that if we are late, we shoudl let someone know
* AI usage is fine, but make sure to look it over yourself before commiting. Then, let your team members know you used AI and make a pull request before merging.
* Be willing to help each other when we are stuck
* Volunteer when you are able to

---

## Decision Making

We will try to reach consensus and/or majority vote as much as possible about deadlines, roles, and any deliverables that would be affecting the project or someone elses work. If someone has an conflict and isn't able to finish something, someone should volunteer to fill the role. Once this happens, this person can not volunteer for another task until everyone else has volunteered. If a similar thing happens, the person who had the conflict must volunteer for the next one.

If someone strongly disagrees, we will do majority vote but aim to have a consensus before this can happen. If two of us are split on one thing and the other two on another, we will try to work together and have a choice made in two days.

---

## Conflict Resolution

By listening to other's opinions and why this conflict exists, voting if necessary, being respectful of others,

---

## Accountability

Follow our norms and expectations, and following the deadlines that people set.
